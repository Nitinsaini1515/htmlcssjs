Calculate a Percentage

Find the percentage of any number quickly and easily.

Formula: (Number × Percentage) ÷ 100

Find What Percentage One Number Is of Another

Calculate what percentage a part represents of the total.

Formula: (Part ÷ Total) × 100

Percentage Increase or Decrease

Calculate how much a value has increased or decreased between two numbers.

Formula: ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Percentage Difference Calculator

Find the percentage difference between two values quickly and easily.

Formula: (|First Value − Second Value| ÷ Average of Both Values) × 100
